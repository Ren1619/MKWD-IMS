# Returns

## Keep the shared RMS document card white
RmsFormView is a white document surface in preparation, decision dialogs and the detail page, including dark mode. Keep table rows white on hover/selection and use explicit dark text, readable secondary labels and light borders inside this card.

## Keep RMS inspection details on a white surface
The Inspection, storage and stock posting details disclosure in Returns/Show uses a white background, including its nested desktop table and mobile material cards. Use readable dark text, muted slate labels, and light borders in both themes.
