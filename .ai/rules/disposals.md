# Disposals

## WMR decisions control witnessed disposal and protect parent assets
New disposal uses frozen WMR lines, independent IMS approval and signed committee authority, followed by inspector, Internal Control and Accounting witness evidence. Only completion posts linked whole-property disposal; standalone removed-component waste never derecognizes its parent. Preserve physical disposition versus posting dates, per-line sale receipts, and Accounting confirmation; do not generate a financial JEV. Generic public event forms cannot post disposal. Manual 009 supplied here repeats the transfer procedure; do not claim a validated disposal SOP from its filename.
