# Assets

## Do not expose property borrowing
MKWD does not use or plan a borrowing workflow. New property movement must use formal accountability, return, relief, or transfer records. Preserve legacy borrowing history and allow legacy active records to be returned, but do not expose a route or UI that creates new borrowings.
